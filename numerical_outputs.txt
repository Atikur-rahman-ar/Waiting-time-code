SCENARIO 1  8-NUCLEOTIDE MICRO-RNA SEED MATCH, BOTH PRESITE REGIMES  (Sec. 6.2)
==============================================================================
PRESITE-RICH (Durrett & Schmidt 2008, QUOTED):
  a 1-kb region holds on average 3.85 six-of-eight partial matches to any target word;
  a seven-of-eight presite exists with probability ~ 5/16.
  8-mer completed from a 7-of-8 presite : 3.75e5 yr   (1.5e4 gen = 0.06 B)
  PWM-flexible (any 7-of-8 binds)       : ~6e4 yr
  de novo 6-mer in a 1-kb region        : ~1e5 yr
  presite-free exact 8-mer (their sim.) : ~650 Myr  (= 108 B)

PRESITE-FREE, single specified locus, COMPUTED via Eq. (3):
  C_8 = 1   (omega_8 = 4^-8)      : T = 5.498e+63 gen  (1.37e+65 yr)
  C_8 = 1e3 (omega_8 = 1.5e-2)    : T = 5.498e+60 gen  (1.37e+62 yr)

SCENARIO 2  DURRETT-SCHMIDT TWO-MUTATION REGULATORY CASE  (QUOTED, Table 2)
==============================================================================
parameters: n = 2e4 haploids; mu = 1e-8; u1 = 1e-7 (inactivation); u2 = 3.3e-9 (creation);
            neutral intermediate; 1-kb region (DS 2008, Table 2)
Theorem 1 mean = 8,660,258 generations = 217 Myr at 25 yr/gen
simulation mean= 6,464,920 generations
excess over B  = 36.1 x

CENSUS SENSITIVITY AXIS (arrival sums over census; establishment local to Ne; structured-population
scaling linear in c = census/Ne; the panmictic alternative would set Ne = census, rejected by the
three genealogical Ne measurements):
  census     1 x Ne: mean = 8.660e+06 gen = 216.506 Myr; excess = 36.0844 x B  (outside budget)
  census    10 x Ne: mean = 8.660e+05 gen = 21.651 Myr; excess = 3.6084 x B  (outside budget)
  census   100 x Ne: mean = 8.660e+04 gen = 2.165 Myr; excess = 0.3608 x B  (within budget)
  census  1000 x Ne: mean = 8.660e+03 gen = 0.217 Myr; excess = 0.0361 x B  (within budget)
plausibility anchor: habitable-area reconstructions for Pleistocene human populations place the
Old World census at 2.1-8.3 million at the Last Glacial Maximum (Gautney & Holliday 2015,
J Archaeol Sci 58:103-112), i.e., sustained census/Ne ratios of ~2e2-8e2 against Ne=1e4 - the
hominin-specific 100x-1000x reading. Across other vertebrate taxa the sustained adult-census/Ne
ratio is typically ~2-10 (Frankham 1995, Genet Res 66:95-107).
establishment phase (fixation of the completed allele, ~4Ne = 1 Myr) is unaffected by census
but is short relative to B in every reading.
the presite-rich variants of the same biology (Scenario 1) run 1e4-6e4 gen and fit the budget

SCENARIO 4 / A.4  DE NOVO BENEFICIAL ORF SUPPLY  (Sec. 5.6, Appendix A.4)
==============================================================================
Nc=1e+05, n_tr=1e+03: trials = 2.40e+13; required establishment-competent density rho_fix = 2.50e-12  [central census]
    s = 1e-02: establishment cost 1/(2s) = 5e+01 -> required origination density = 1.25e-10
    s = 1e-03: establishment cost 1/(2s) = 5e+02 -> required origination density = 1.25e-09
Nc=1e+06, n_tr=1e+04: trials = 2.40e+15; required establishment-competent density rho_fix = 2.50e-14  [generous census]
    s = 1e-02: establishment cost 1/(2s) = 5e+01 -> required origination density = 1.25e-12
    s = 1e-03: establishment cost 1/(2s) = 5e+02 -> required origination density = 1.25e-11
benchmark densities (QUOTED):
  Keefe-Szostak 2001: 4 ATP-binding 80-mers recovered from ~6e12 random sequences
                      -> rho ~ 6.7e-13, quoted in the literature as order 1e-12 to 1e-11
  Axe 2004           : rho <= 1e-77 for a specified 150-residue structured fold with function
  -> origination requirement coincides with the generic-binding anchor at the generous census
     reading (marginally supplyable), exceeds it at the central reading, and is tens of orders
     ABOVE what Axe-class specificity would require (excluded if Axe-class) under every reading
NOTE: the naive model T = 1/(2Ne mu^3 n_ORF rho) is NOT used in the manuscript; it double-counts
specification by billing ORF origination itself to the standard model.
